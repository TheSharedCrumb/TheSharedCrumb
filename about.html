Hi, I'm Danielle

Easy, healthier dinners for family and friends, cooked in a real kitchen with a toddler underfoot.

Why The Shared Crumb?

I've always been the one cooking for family and friends. To me, food is meant to be shared, whether that's a quick weeknight dinner at home or a dumpling night with a kitchen full of people. That's where the name comes from.

After I had my baby, I wanted to change a few things. I wanted to get healthier, save money, and pay closer attention to what I was actually putting into my body. Cooking at home more turned out to be the answer to all three.

These are the recipes that made the cut: easy, healthier dinners that don't cost much and are good enough to share. Every recipe is something I cook at home, and my toddler is my toughest critic. Toddler-friendly recipes and tips are on the way, too.

🥗 Easy & Healthier

Simple weeknight dinners made with real ingredients from any grocery store. Most are on the table in 45 minutes or less.

💰 Budget-Friendly

Cooking at home saves real money. Every recipe has a cost rating, compared to the other recipes here:

  • $ Everyday ingredients and cheaper proteins
  • $$ A pricier protein or a specialty item or two
  • $$$ A splurge, or a big batch for a crowd

🍽️ Made to Share

Recipes I've cooked for the people I love. If a dish isn't worth sharing, it doesn't go on the site.
