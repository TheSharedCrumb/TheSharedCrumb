### Ingredients
* 1 cup Unsalted Butter (browned and cooled)
* 1 cup Dark Brown Sugar
* 1/2 cup Granulated White Sugar
* 2 Large Eggs (room temperature)
* 2 tsp Vanilla Extract
* 2 1/4 cups All-Purpose Flour
* 1 tsp Baking Soda
* 1 tsp Sea Salt
* 1 1/2 cups Semi-Sweet Chocolate Chunk Chips

### Instructions
1. **Brown the Butter**: Melt butter in a saucepan over medium heat, swirling until foam subsides and dark golden flecks form. Pour into a bowl and let cool for 10 minutes.
2. **Mix Wet & Dry**: Whisk brown butter, brown sugar, and white sugar together. Beat in eggs and vanilla until pale and thick. Fold in flour, baking soda, and salt, then stir in chocolate chunks.
3. **Chill & Bake**: Chill dough for at least 30 minutes. Scoop onto lined baking sheets and bake at 375°F (190°C) for 10-12 minutes until edges are golden. Sprinkle with flaky sea salt.
