### Ingredients
* 1 cup Unsalted Butter (browned and cooled slightly)
* 3/4 cup Dark Brown Sugar
* 1/2 cup Granulated White Sugar
* 1 Large Egg + 1 Egg Yolk
* 1 tsp Vanilla Extract
* 2 1/4 cups All-Purpose Flour
* 1 tsp Baking Soda
* 1/2 tsp Sea Salt
* 1 1/2 cups Semi-Sweet Chocolate Chunks

### Instructions
1. **Brown Butter**: Melt butter over medium heat, swirling until golden brown and nutty-smelling (~5 mins). Cool for 10 mins.
2. **Mix Wet & Dry**: Whisk browned butter with sugars, egg, yolk, and vanilla until smooth. Fold in flour, baking soda, salt, and chocolate chunks.
3. **Bake**: Chill dough for 30 mins. Scoop 2-tbsp balls onto a parchment-lined tray and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 10-12 minutes until edges are golden.
