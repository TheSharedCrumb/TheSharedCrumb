### Ingredients
* 500g Bread Flour
* 350g Water
* 100g Active Starter
* 10g Salt

### Instructions
1. **Mix**: Combine flour, water, and active starter. Let rest (autolyse) for 30 minutes.
2. **Knead & Fold**: Add salt and perform stretch-and-folds every 30 minutes for 2 hours.
3. **Bake**: Preheat oven with a Dutch oven at 450°F (230°C). Score and bake covered for 20 mins, then uncovered for 20 mins.
