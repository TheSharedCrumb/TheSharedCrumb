### Ingredients
* 500g Bread Flour
* 350g Water (70% hydration)
* 100g Active Sourdough Starter
* 10g Fine Sea Salt

### Instructions
1. **Autolyse**: Mix flour and water until no dry bits remain. Cover and rest for 45 minutes.
2. **Incorporate Starter & Salt**: Fold in active starter and salt until fully integrated.
3. **Bulk Fermentation**: Perform 4 sets of stretch-and-folds spaced 30 minutes apart. Let rise until expanded by 50%.
4. **Shape & Bake**: Shape into a boule, place in a proofing basket, and chill overnight. Bake in a preheated Dutch oven at 450°F (230°C) with lid on for 20 mins, then lid off for 25 mins.
