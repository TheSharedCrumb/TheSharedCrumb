Sticky Sweet Salmon Fillets

### Ingredients
* 4 (6oz) salmon filets
* ½ tsp salt
* ½ tsp black pepper
* ½ tsp paprika
* ¼ tsp cajun seasoning
* 3 Tbsp butter
* 2 tsp olive oil
* 6 cloves of garlic minced
* ½ cup honey
* 3 tbsp water
* 3 tbsp soy sauce
* 2 tbsp lemon juice

### Instructions
1. **Seasoning**: Mix together salt, black pepper, paprika, and cajun seasoning. Pat dry salmon and sprinkle seasoning on salmon filets
2. **Sauce**: In a large oven safe skillet over medium high heat, add butter. When melted add garlic, water, soy sauce, honey, and lemon juice. 
3. **Salmon**: Once boiling add salmon (skin side down if your filets have skin). Baste consistently with sauce for 3 minutes while cooking.
4. **Bake**: Bake in the oven for 5-6 minutes at 450 degrees until the glaze beings to carmelize
5. **Serve**: Serve with rice and your favorite vegetable.
