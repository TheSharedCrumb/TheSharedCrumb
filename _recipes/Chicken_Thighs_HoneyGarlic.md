There are nights when cooking feels like an Olympic sport, and then there are nights when you just need something fast, sticky, and deeply comforting to save the evening. This honey garlic chicken is my absolute go-to when the clock is ticking, the pantry looks sparse, and nobody has the patience for a complicated recipe.

It all comes down to that glorious moment in the skillet when the garlic hits the melted butter, soy sauce, and honey. The whole kitchen instantly fills with that rich, sweet, and savory aroma that makes everyone casually wander into the kitchen to ask when dinner will be ready.

Searing the chicken first locks in all the juices, but the real magic happens in the oven. As the thighs bake, that garlic-honey glaze bubbles up, thickens, and lacquers onto the crispy skin into a golden, sticky coating.

Spoon it over a steamy bed of white rice or thick noodles to catch every last drop of extra sauce. It is fast, foolproof, and tastes like you spent hours standing over a hot stove instead of just throwing a few kitchen staples together in under forty minutes.

### Ingredients
* 6 chicken thighs about 5oz each
* Salt pepper to taste
* 2 tbsp of butter divided
* 6 cloves of garlic diced or crushed
* 3 tbsp honey
* 2 tbsp soy sauce
* ½ tsp ground thyme
* ½ tsp ground ginger

### Instructions
1. **Chicken**: Pat the chicken thighs thoroughly dry with paper towels, then season both sides with salt and pepper. Melt 1 tablespoon of butter in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and sear for about 5 minutes per side until golden brown.
2. **Sauce**: Transfer the seared chicken onto a rimmed baking sheet. Reduce the skillet heat to medium, then add the remaining 1 tablespoon of butter, garlic, honey, soy sauce, thyme, and ginger. 
3. **Thicken**: Whisk the sauce continuously for 1 to 2 minutes, scraping up any browned bits from the pan, until glossy and slightly thickened. Spoon half of the glaze over the chicken thighs.
4. **Bake**: Bake at 400°F (200°C) for 20 minutes, flipping the chicken and brushing with the remaining glaze halfway through, until the internal temperature reaches 165°F.
5. **Serve**: Spoon any extra pan juices over the top and serve hot over rice or noodles.
