Chicken Marsala is a classic Italian-American favorite that balances crisp, golden chicken with the deep, earthy richness of sautéed mushrooms and Marsala wine. By searing thin cutlets quickly in butter and olive oil, you create a delicious browned fond in the pan that serves as the foundation for a velvety cream sauce.

Cooking the mushrooms down with garlic, shallots, and fresh thyme lifts all those rich flavors into the wine reduction. As the sauce simmers, it turns into a silky glaze that coats every cutlet. Serve it hot alongside garlic mashed potatoes, buttered egg noodles, or crusty bread to mop up every drop of extra sauce.

### Ingredients

#### Chicken & Dredge
* 1½ lbs boneless, skinless chicken breasts (or tenders), _pounded ¼-inch thick_
* 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
* 1 tbsp olive oil
* 3 tbsp unsalted butter, _divided_
* Salt and freshly ground black pepper, _to taste_

#### Mushroom Sauce
* 8 oz cremini or button mushrooms, _sliced_
* 1 medium shallot, _finely chopped_
* 2 cloves garlic, _minced_
* ⅔ cup chicken broth
* ⅔ cup dry Marsala wine
* ⅔ cup heavy cream
* 2 tsp fresh thyme, _chopped_

#### For Serving (Optional)
* 2 tbsp fresh Italian parsley, _chopped (for garnish)_
* Pasta, Mashed potatoes, or rice

> **Note on Chicken Prep:** If working with large chicken breasts, slice them in half horizontally to create four thin fillets before pounding them evenly to ¼-inch thickness.

### Instructions
1. **Dredge Chicken:** Combine the flour with a pinch of salt and pepper in a sealable bag. Add the chicken cutlets, seal, and shake until each piece is lightly and evenly coated.
2. **Sear Cutlets:** Heat the olive oil and 2 tablespoons of butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Shake off any excess flour from the chicken and sear for 2 to 3 minutes per side until golden brown and cooked through. Transfer to a plate.
3. **Sauté Mushrooms & Aromatics:** Melt the remaining 1 tablespoon of butter in the same pan over medium heat. Add the sliced mushrooms and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until browned. Stir in the chopped shallot, minced garlic, and a pinch of salt, cooking for 1 to 2 minutes until fragrant.
4. **Simmer Sauce:** Pour in the chicken broth, dry Marsala wine, heavy cream, and fresh thyme. Use a wooden spoon to scrape up any flavorful browned bits stuck to the bottom of the pan. Bring to a gentle boil, then simmer uncovered for 10 to 15 minutes until reduced by half and slightly thickened.
5. **Combine & Finish:** Return the seared chicken and any accumulated juices back to the skillet. Simmer on low heat for 2 to 3 minutes so the chicken warms through and the sauce coats the meat.
6. **Garnish & Serve:** Sprinkle with fresh chopped parsley and serve hot over mashed potatoes or pasta.
