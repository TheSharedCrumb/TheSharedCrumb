### Ingredients
* 1 cup Frozen Mixed Berries
* 1/2 cup Greek Yogurt
* 1 cup Unsweetened Almond Milk
* 1 scoop Vanilla Protein Powder
* 1 tbsp Chia Seeds
* 1 tbsp Honey or Maple Syrup (optional)

### Instructions
1. **Combine**: Add almond milk, Greek yogurt, frozen berries, protein powder, and chia seeds to a high-speed blender.
2. **Blend**: Blend on high for 60 seconds until creamy and completely smooth.
3. **Serve**: Pour into a glass and top with additional chia seeds or berries.
