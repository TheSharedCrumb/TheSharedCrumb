### Ingredients
* 2 thick slices Sourdough Bread
* 1 ripe Avocado
* 2 Fresh Eggs
* 1 tbsp White Vinegar
* 1/2 Lemon (juiced)
* Everything Bagel Seasoning & Red Pepper Flakes
* Salt and Black Pepper to taste

### Instructions
1. **Prepare Avocado**: Mash avocado with lemon juice, salt, and pepper in a small bowl until smooth with light chunks.
2. **Poach Eggs**: Bring a pot of water to a simmer, add vinegar, and create a gentle whirlpool. Drop in eggs and cook for 3 minutes.
3. **Assemble**: Toast sourdough slices, spread avocado generously, top with poached eggs, and sprinkle with seasonings.
