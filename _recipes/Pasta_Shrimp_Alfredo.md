This garlic shrimp fettuccine is the perfect quick-fix dinner when you want something rich and comforting without spending hours over a hot stove. Succulent shrimp and fragrant garlic simmer together before building a silky, fresh Parmesan cream sauce right in the same pan.

As the hot fettuccine gets tossed through the sauce, it coats every strand for a rich, restaurant-quality meal that comes together in under twenty minutes.

### Ingredients
* 1 lb cooked shrimp, _peeled and deveined_
* 1 lb fettuccine pasta
* 1 tbsp butter
* 4 cloves garlic, _minced_
* 1 cup heavy cream (or half-and-half)
* 6 tbsp Parmigiano-Reggiano or Parmesan cheese, _grated_
* 1 tbsp fresh parsley, _chopped_
* Salt, _to taste_

### Instructions
1. **Cook Pasta:** Bring a large pot of salted water to a rolling boil. Add the fettuccine and cook according to package directions until al dente (about 8 minutes). Drain and set aside.
2. **Sauté Shrimp & Garlic:** Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the shrimp and minced garlic, sautéing for about 1 minute until fragrant.
3. **Build Sauce:** Pour in the heavy cream and stir to combine. Gradually add the grated Parmesan cheese, about 1 tablespoon at a time, whisking constantly until smooth and melted. 
4. **Thicken:** Stir in the fresh chopped parsley and season with a pinch of salt. Simmer gently over medium-low heat, stirring frequently, until the sauce thickens slightly.
5. **Combine & Serve:** Add the cooked fettuccine directly into the skillet. Toss continuously until the pasta is evenly coated in the warm cream sauce. Serve immediately.
