### Ingredients
* 2 cans (15 oz) Chickpeas (drained and rinsed)
* 1 can (14 oz) Full-Fat Coconut Milk
* 1 Yellow Onion (diced)
* 3 cloves Garlic (minced)
* 1 tbsp Fresh Ginger (grated)
* 2 tbsp Yellow Curry Powder
* 2 cups Fresh Spinach
* Jasmine Rice for serving

### Instructions
1. **Sauté Aromatics**: Heat oil in a deep skillet over medium heat. Add onion, garlic, and ginger; cook for 4-5 minutes until soft.
2. **Simmer Curry**: Stir in curry powder, chickpeas, and coconut milk. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook for 12-15 minutes until thickened.
3. **Finish & Serve**: Stir in spinach until wilted. Serve hot over steamed jasmine rice with fresh cilantro.
