Privacy Policy

Last updated September 26, 2026

The Shared Crumb is a personal recipe site. There are no accounts, and I don't collect your name or email unless you email me.

Visits

I use Google Analytics, which uses cookies, to see how many people visit and which recipes they read. I only see totals, not who you are. Google handles this data under its privacy policy. The site's fonts and icons load from Google and Cloudflare, so those services see your IP address like any website would.

Email

If you email me, I'll only use your address to reply. I won't share it or add it to a list.

Your choices

You can block cookies in your browser or opt out of Google Analytics with Google's add-on. Links to Pinterest, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube follow those sites' own policies.

Questions

Email me at Hello@TheSharedCrumb.com. If anything here changes, I'll update this page.
